# £600 purchase thesis and two Cold Demo directions

**Status:** Design research only. No code, images, Demo or public page was created.

## £600 Purchase Thesis

For a homeowner or property decision-maker arriving with a window, door, repair or bespoke-glass need, City Glass’s public evidence already supplies the ingredients for trust: a long-running local company identity, real branded fleet, broad verified services, Edinburgh/Lothian coverage, a public trading profile and a direct quote/enquiry route. A £600 one-page concept would be worth investigating only if it turns those ingredients into a faster first decision: **which kind of job is mine, why is this a credible local team, and where do I request a quote?**

- **Five-second recognition:** an original hero must read as Edinburgh glazing/joinery work, not a generic home-improvement site. Use verified service language and only authorised/cleared visual material.
- **Why it belongs to City Glass:** service range, Edinburgh/Lothian coverage, branded fleet as research evidence and Trusted Trader presence distinguish the direction. None is a licence to reuse the underlying photos or logo.
- **Commercial increment:** replaces a pale image-led first screen and long service menu with an immediate, customer-job-led route to existing contact/quote intent.
- **Hero to bottom 40%:** job selection → relevant proof/service detail → what happens next → existing contact route, with no fake form, booking or guarantee.

**Limits:** £600 is a research hypothesis requested for this UK candidate. No public evidence verifies the company’s budget, desire to change the site, purchase intent, or marketing consent.

## Direction A — Edinburgh glazing, clearly chosen (recommended)

**Hero composition:** an original, single dedicated concept image of light/architectural glazing (not a fake City Glass project) with a strong black/white/red-inspired information rail. Headline groups three verified needs: windows & heritage joinery; doors & repairs; bespoke glass.

**Service slice:**

1. Windows & heritage joinery — UPVC and sash/case service context only where verified.
2. Doors & glass repairs — repairs and door services.
3. Bespoke architectural glass — balustrades, splashbacks and roof/cupola-related work only as verified.

**Bottom 40%:** compact “what to include in an enquiry” guidance (job type, property/location, measurements/photos optional only if customer confirms) followed by a single external link to the existing contact page. It does not create a submission flow.

**Mobile:** one stacked job choice per row, contact intent always visible after service choice; no tiny social-only header, no reliance on hover or dense menu.

**Motion:** intensity 1/5: a single optional opacity/position entrance with reduced-motion fallback. It is not needed to carry business meaning.

**Why recommended:** it serves the site’s broad verified service mix without pretending all visitors need a premium architectural-glass project.

## Direction B — Measure, make, fit

**Hero composition:** a quiet, text-led sequence framing the customer’s decision as `Repair / Replace / Make something bespoke`, paired with one original detail-oriented glass/measurement concept image.

**Service slice:** repair/replace/bespoke routes, each landing on a verified service description and a direct existing enquiry route.

**Bottom 40%:** an original three-stage enquiry explanation: tell us the need → arrange the appropriate next contact/survey step only if verified → receive a quote. No time, price, guarantee, response-time or survey claim without evidence.

**Mobile:** prominent vertical decision flow; each card is a large text-first tap target with supporting detail rather than a photo mosaic.

**Motion:** intensity 0–1/5; prefer static clarity.

**Trade-off:** stronger for repair/replacement intent, but risks underrepresenting the company’s visually distinctive balustrade/splashback offer. Select it only after a future Owner verifies that repair/replacement is the desired commercial priority.
