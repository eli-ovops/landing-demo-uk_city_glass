## Purchase-process reading

**Fact:** the owned site invites quote/survey and general enquiries; current Trusted Trader examples describe domestic window, door, secondary-glazing and repair work.  
**Inference:** homeowners or property decision-makers commonly need to identify the relevant job, gain trust from local proof, then request a survey/quote. A concept should make those three stages visible rather than promise leads, savings, speed or outcomes.  
**UNKNOWN:** actual lead sources, close rate, budget, owner identity, marketing consent, priority service mix and whether the business wants new work.

## Material risk evidence

Trusted Trader’s recent feed contains both positive and critical feedback. The report may state the platform score and the existence of current work, but must not turn selected reviews into universal service, pricing or quality claims. Any testimonial used later requires its own rights/consent and accuracy check.
